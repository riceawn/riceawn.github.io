# Privacy Policy for Sudoku: Pieces

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

Sudoku: Pieces is a puzzle game. This policy explains local game storage, advertising, purchases and optional data choices.

## 1. Versions and local game data

Versions 1.0.3 and earlier were paid, offline releases without an advertising or analytics SDK. Version 1.0.4 introduced ad-supported access and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. iOS version 1.0.5 includes banner and interstitial advertisements.

Puzzle progress, records, sound preferences and language choices are saved locally on your device. The game does not require an account or provide cloud synchronization of your puzzle progress. Uninstalling the app may delete locally saved records. Puzzles can be played offline; advertising, purchases and purchase restoration may require an internet connection.

## 2. Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-supported releases use Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, crash reports, performance information and other diagnostics. This information may be used to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and improve services, subject to your choices and applicable requirements.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents advertising privacy choices. You can revisit these under **Settings → Advertising choices** when that entry is available. UMP consent does not itself grant Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. The current iOS app does not request access to the advertising identifier through ATT.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 3. Purchases and ad-free access

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app checks store entitlements to provide and restore ad-free access. We do not receive your payment card details. **Restore purchases** is available in Settings.

Ad-free access removes both banner and interstitial advertisements. The updated iOS app also skips advertising initialization and ad loading while ad-free access is active. Earlier paid-owner eligibility is determined through store purchase information.

## 4. Optional analytics and crash reports

The Android app and iOS version 1.0.5 (Build 8) and later offer separate optional Google Firebase usage statistics and crash reports. They are disabled until you opt in, and can be changed under **Settings → Data choices**. These choices are separate from advertising privacy preferences.

Usage statistics may include puzzle difficulty, progress, completion, advertising events and purchase outcomes. Crash reports may include error details, app state, device information and app installation identifiers. Turning off a choice stops future collection under that choice; it does not automatically delete previously submitted information. Earlier iOS builds do not include these optional Firebase features. Firebase Analytics on iOS is configured without advertising identifier support and with vendor identifier collection disabled. Optional usage statistics are not used for personalized advertising.

## 5. Children

The ad-supported app includes advertisements and purchases and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The game does not ask players for their name, email, date of birth or contacts. If you believe a child's information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

## 6. Your choices and contact

You can revisit advertising choices where available, disable optional data collection, restore purchases, or remove locally saved game data by uninstalling the app. For privacy questions, including questions about information already submitted, contact **biaaaa.games@gmail.com** or use the [project support page](https://github.com/tianyulu1013/sudokublocks/issues). Do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the current version.

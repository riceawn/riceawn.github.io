## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I steer the ball?
Use the **◀ Left** and **▶ Right** steering buttons at the bottom of the screen to influence your ball's momentum. Tap your equipped tactical ability (such as **Downward Dash**) to activate speed boosts or clear surrounding competitors.

### 2. Can I play Skillball Rally offline?
Yes. Skillball Rally's physics simulation and custom workshop run completely locally on your device with no internet connection required.

### 3. How does the "Remove Ads" purchase work?
Skillball Rally offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase to permanently disable interstitial advertisements. If you switch devices or reinstall the game, your purchase status can be restored automatically.

### 4. What should I do if performance stutters?
Ensure your device is not operating in Low Power Mode and close intensive background apps. The physics engine is optimized for 60 FPS across all supported mobile devices.

### 5. Where can I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Please email details including your device model, OS version, and a description to **biaaaa.games@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information Collection & Advertising

- **No Personal Account Required**: We do not require registration, login, or personal contact details to play the Game.
- **Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)**: The Game displays mobile advertisements served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and device identifiers (such as Advertising ID or IDFA) to serve relevant ads, prevent ad fraud, and measure ad performance in compliance with applicable platform privacy requirements.
  - Learn more about Google's privacy practices: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **In-App Purchases**: In-App Purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed securely by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store payment card details.

### Firebase gameplay statistics and crash diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the Game is used and to identify and fix technical problems. These services are separate from permission for advertising tracking.

- **Firebase Analytics** may process installation and session information, gameplay events such as round starts and completions, advertisement availability/results, app-instance identifiers, and device/app information. We do not send player names, custom photos, payment details, or raw JavaScript error messages as gameplay events. Where prior consent is required, Analytics collection follows your Analytics consent choice, gathered through our privacy message or a specific statistics prompt. Where prior consent is not required by the applicable policy, basic non-advertising measurement can be enabled automatically. You can turn gameplay statistics off separately in the Game's Settings. We do not enable Firebase advertising personalization or collect IDFA through our Firebase Analytics integration.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** processes crash traces, limited error diagnostics, device/operating-system/app information, and Crashlytics/Firebase installation identifiers to detect failures and improve reliability. For this limited non-advertising diagnostic purpose, crash reporting is enabled by default unless you have opted out, subject to applicable consent requirements. You can turn crash reports off separately in Settings. Disabling this option stops future reporting and deletes unsent crash reports held by the Game.
- **Retention and choices**: Google states that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from its live and backup systems. Analytics data retention follows the configured Google Analytics retention controls; aggregated reports may be retained longer. Turning a setting off stops future collection but does not automatically erase previously processed data. Contact us at the address below for questions or requests about retention or deletion. Existing explicit refusals are preserved when the Game is updated.

Google processes these records according to its [Firebase privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and applicable data-processing terms. The collection described here does not require a game account.

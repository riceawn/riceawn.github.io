# Mahjong Mixup — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Mahjong Mixup (“the App”) is developed and published by Rice Awn LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of the App.

## Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera access, microphone access, precise location, or payment information.

The App may store certain preferences locally on your device, such as tile style and sound settings. This information is not uploaded to our servers and may be removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements on both iOS and Android.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

- Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
- IP address
- Information about ad impressions and interactions
- Device and diagnostic information
- Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention

Depending on your location, device settings, and applicable laws, Google may show personalized, non-personalized, limited, or contextual advertisements.

Google processes this information according to its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Unity Ads on iOS

The iOS version may show ads from Unity Ads through Google AdMob mediation. When Unity Ads is used, Unity may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address or approximate location, device and connection information, ad views and interactions, and usage and performance information. Unity uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate its services, and help prevent fraud. The information processed depends on the ad request, your settings and permissions, and applicable law.

Unity explains its data practices, retention, and privacy choices in its [Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## Analytics and Crash Reporting on iOS

The iOS version uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. It records events such as starting or finishing a game, completing the tutorial, and whether a purchase attempt was completed. Analytics may process app or device identifiers and app, device, or session information to measure usage and improve the App.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix crashes. Crash reports may include a crash trace, the App version, device and operating system information, and an installation identifier. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) for more information.

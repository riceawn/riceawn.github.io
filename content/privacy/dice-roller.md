# Dice Roller: 3D Simulator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Provider: **Rice Awn LLC**  
Contact: **contact@riceawn.com**

Rice Awn LLC provides Dice Roller: 3D Simulator. This policy explains how the app and its service providers handle information. Features and privacy controls may differ by app version and platform.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores settings, dice selections, appearance choices, gameplay preferences and cached purchase entitlements on your device.

You do not need to create an app account or provide your name to roll dice. Operating-system backup services may include app data according to your device settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove local settings. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored through the relevant store account.

## Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner, rewarded and interstitial ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process device and app information, identifiers where permitted, IP address, diagnostics and ad interactions to deliver, measure and protect advertising services.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy messages where required. Available advertising choices can be revisited through Settings → Privacy & data → Advertising privacy (when required).

Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads can be displayed. Purchased ad-removal benefits apply to the placements described on the purchase page.

## Usage analytics

In versions using the updated measurement controls, basic usage analytics is enabled by default. Firebase Analytics is configured with analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization consent denied. In this restricted mode, Google describes measurement events as being sent without user or device identifiers. We do not set user IDs or add personal information to these events.

Events describe feature use, such as modes entered, dice counts and advertising interactions. Network requests still involve technical information such as an IP address and device/app information; this should not be interpreted as a promise that no information is transmitted. Google's processing and retention practices apply.

You can turn Usage analytics off in Settings → Privacy & data. An existing choice to turn analytics off remains off after an update. Advertising eligibility does not grant analytics tracking permission. We do not enable analytics or advertising identifier storage through this switch.

Older versions may instead leave analytics disabled until selected and may use app-instance identifiers when it is enabled. The privacy controls and SDK configuration of the version you use apply.

Disabling analytics stops future app analytics collection controlled by this setting. It does not automatically delete information already processed by the provider.

## Optional crash reports

App versions with Firebase Crashlytics provide a separate Crash reports choice. This choice defaults to off until selected.

If enabled, Crashlytics can process crash stack traces, diagnostic information, device and operating-system information, app version and the current app mode to help us identify and fix defects.

We do not deliberately add your name, email address, payment details or dice-result history to crash reports.

You can disable Crash reports in Settings → Privacy & data. Disabling reporting stops future report uploads controlled by this setting. Reports already submitted remain subject to the provider’s policies and applicable retention settings. Diagnostic files may exist locally even when automatic uploading is disabled.

## Purchases

Google Play or the Apple App Store processes purchases available on its platform. Unity In-App Purchasing helps the app retrieve product information, check purchases, confirm transactions and restore access.

These services process product identifiers, transaction and receipt information, purchase status and technical information needed to operate and protect the purchase service. The app stores a local entitlement cache for offline access.

Rice Awn LLC does not receive your full payment-card information through the in-app purchase interface.

Analytics and crash-reporting choices do not prevent the essential processing needed to complete or restore a purchase.

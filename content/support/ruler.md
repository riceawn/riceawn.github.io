### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### 1. How does the app guarantee 1:1 actual-size accuracy?
Our app utilizes true hardware pixel-density mapping tailored to your specific device model. Unlike camera-based tools, 1 centimeter or 1 inch on your screen corresponds strictly to physical measurements in the real world.

#### 2. How do I calibrate using a card?
1. Open **Settings** (gear icon in the top capsule).
2. Tap **Credit / Debit Card Calibration**.
3. Place any standard credit card, debit card, or driver's license vertically over the dashed guide.
4. Use the `+` or `-` buttons until the on-screen outline matches your physical card's edges.
5. Tap **Save Calibration**. Your calibration ratio will be stored locally.

#### 3. How do I use Caliper Mode?
Tap the **📐 Caliper** button in the floating control capsule at the top of the screen (or simply touch the screen with two fingers). Two adjustable horizontal jaws will appear with a live dimension arrow and thickness readout. Drag either jaw to measure objects with precision.

#### 4. How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?
If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new device:
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **"Already purchased? Restore"**.
3. Your previous purchase will be validated with the App Store and ad-free status will be reactivated immediately.

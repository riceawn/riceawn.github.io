Website privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Visiting riceawn.com

This website is operated by Rice Awn LLC and hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, account registration, or contact forms to this website.

GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contacting us

If you email contact@riceawn.com, we receive your email address and the information you send. Our domain email forwarding service and Gmail process the correspondence to deliver and store it. We use it to respond to you and keep records as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information. You can contact us to ask about information held in support correspondence.

App privacy

Each app has its own data practices. Please use the app privacy directory to find the relevant policy.

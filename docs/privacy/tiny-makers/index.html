PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Tiny Makers: Kids Art & Play

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

This Privacy Policy applies to Tiny Makers: Kids Art & Play, displayed as Tiny Makers on your device. The app provides creative activities for children and their parents or guardians.

Contact: contact@riceawn.com

Artwork and app settings

Sticker scenes, drawings, coloring pages, paper cuts, custom stickers and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload these creations to our servers or provide in-app cloud synchronization. Neither you nor your child needs to create an account.

You can delete saved creations through My Art. Deleting a paper cut also removes its corresponding item from My Stickers. Images already used in other saved pictures remain available so those pictures stay intact.

Deleting the app may remove its locally saved data. Device backups and restores, if enabled, are controlled by your operating system and account settings.

In-app purchases

Apple processes purchases in the iPhone and iPad app. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to verify access to purchased content. We do not receive your payment card details.

Restoring a purchase restores access to purchased content, not artwork stored on another device.

Apple handles App Store account, transaction and related information under its own privacy practices:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Advertising and analytics

The app does not contain advertising, third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not use the app to track children across apps or websites, or sell their personal information.

Apple may provide diagnostics or other platform services according to your device and account settings.

Support communications

If you email contact@riceawn.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Please do not send children's names, photos, voices or artwork.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant support records. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Children's privacy and parent controls

The app lets children play without registering an account or submitting personal information to us.

Purchasing opportunities and external links are placed in the parent area behind an adult question. Apple's purchase authentication, parental restrictions and Ask to Buy settings operate separately from this question.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact contact@riceawn.com so we can review the message and arrange appropriate deletion.

External links

When a parent opens a policy or support link, the website or hosting service handles that visit under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will revise the date above when we make changes. The latest version will be available through the privacy policy link in the app and its App Store listing.

PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Book of Answers

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Book of Answers is provided by Rice Awn LLC for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app. For privacy questions, contact contact@riceawn.com.

Your answers and app data

The app selects answers from content included with the app. It does not ask you to create an account or upload your questions or chosen answers to a Rice Awn server. The app stores your remove-ads purchase status on your device for quick access and checks the current purchase with Google Play or Apple App Store. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device settings.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may process your IP address, device and advertising identifiers where available, app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Your choices and location can affect which ads can be shown and which identifiers may be used. We use Google’s User Messaging Platform to present required privacy choices. When a privacy-choice option is required, you can reopen it from the app’s Settings. On iOS, system tracking permission may also be requested if an IDFA message is enabled; declining it does not stop the app from working. Removing ads stops this app from requesting ads, but it does not remove the purchase or analytics services described below.

Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use and improve the experience. The app records when the cover or an answer is shown, which numbered answer was viewed, how many times the book was opened in a session, how long a page was read, and whether an ad impression occurred. We do not include the text of your question or answer in these events. Firebase may also process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and diagnostic data to provide analytics. The app sets consent defaults before Firebase starts and uses the available privacy choices to update applicable consent settings. Events that occur while the app is still waiting for a required choice are not saved for later upload.

See Firebase privacy and security.

Purchases

A one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play on Android or Apple App Store on iOS. The app receives product and transaction status so it can apply and restore your purchase. It does not receive your payment-card number. The store may handle your account and payment information under its own policies. If you restore a purchase, the app asks the relevant store for current ownership. Refunds and purchase records are handled by the store.

Retention and your choices

The app’s local purchase cache remains until the store reports that you no longer own the purchase or app data is removed. Google and the app stores retain information under their own policies and legal obligations. We do not have a user account or a separate server holding a copy of your questions. You can use the app’s Settings to reopen available ad privacy choices, restore a purchase, or contact us about a privacy request. Device settings also provide controls for advertising identifiers and tracking. Uninstalling the app removes its local data subject to platform backup behavior; it does not cancel or refund a store purchase.

Security and children

Data sent to Google services and the app stores is protected in transit by those services. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app or a support email, please contact us so we can review the request.

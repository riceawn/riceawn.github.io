PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Tear-Off Notes

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Tear-Off Notes (“the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information.

Android package: com.riceawngames.tearoffnotes
Privacy contact: contact@riceawn.com

1. Your Notes and Local Storage

Tear-Off Notes stores your notebook names, written notes, drawings, page progress, relevant dates, and preferences locally on your device. This information is used to provide notebook functionality and restore your progress when you reopen the app.

The app does not require an account and does not upload your notebook content to our servers or send it to advertising providers.

Your device’s operating system may include app data in backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. Those services are controlled by your operating system provider, not by us.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob, including the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display advertisements. It also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices where applicable.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share:

  • IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Interaction information, including app launches and interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information concerning app and advertising SDK performance.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data processing may vary according to your region, consent choices, device settings, and advertising configuration. Non-personalized advertising may still involve processing information for these purposes.

We do not provide your written notes or drawings to Google for advertising.

For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Information You Send to Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information to answer your questions, provide support, and address privacy requests.

Please avoid sending sensitive notebook content unless it is necessary for your request.

4. Your Choices

You can edit or delete notes and delete notebooks within the app. You can remove locally stored app data through Android’s app settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may permanently remove your local notebooks.

Previously created operating system backups may remain until you remove them through your device or backup provider’s settings.

You can manage advertising identifiers through Android’s privacy settings. Where available or required, the app provides advertising consent controls and a Privacy choices option in Settings.

5. Data Retention

Local notebook data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app’s stored data.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Information processed by Google is subject to Google’s policies and retention practices.

6. Security

The app uses its application storage for local notebook data. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protecting access to your device and managing its backup settings also helps protect your notes.

7. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us to make a request concerning information we hold. We cannot remotely access or delete notebooks stored only on your device. Information held by Google can also be managed through Google’s privacy tools.

9. External Links

The app may open third-party websites, including model attribution links and advertising destinations. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

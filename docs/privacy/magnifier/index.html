PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Magnifier: Reading Loupe

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

This policy explains how Magnifier: Reading Loupe handles information on Android and iOS. Features, advertising formats, and service integrations may vary by platform and release.

1. Camera and images

The app uses your device camera to provide live magnification, a front-camera mirror, freeze-frame review, and image adjustments. Camera frames are processed on your device. We do not upload your camera preview or saved images to our servers or send their contents to advertising or analytics providers.

When you choose to save an image, the app can store it in its local saved-images collection or export it to your device photo library. Saved-image titles, categories, creation dates, and file references remain in local app storage. The app also stores local records needed to remember free saves and advertising rewards.

The saved-images collection is local app storage; its name does not imply a separate encrypted storage service. Protect access to your device, particularly if you save images of labels, documents, or other personal material.

2. Device permissions

Camera permission enables magnification and the mirror. The rear camera flashlight can illuminate objects. Photo-library or storage permission, where required by your operating system, enables saving images you choose to export.

You can manage these permissions in device settings. Refusing or withdrawing a permission may prevent the corresponding feature from working. The app does not record audio for its magnifying or mirror features.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads that can unlock saving benefits.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, app and device information, interactions such as ad views or taps, and diagnostic information. These services use information to deliver and measure advertising, maintain performance, and prevent fraud. An IP address may be used to estimate general location.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides advertising privacy messages and choices where applicable. When required and available in your version, use the app’s Privacy Settings control to revisit advertising choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and tracking permissions in Android or iOS settings.

Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve processing technical information. Advertising choices do not necessarily disable separate analytics services.

4. Firebase analytics and diagnostics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve the experience. Depending on the release and service configuration, this may include app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, app launches, usage events, and general geographic information. Such identifiers and events are not necessarily anonymous.

Releases that include Firebase Crashlytics may process installation identifiers, crash stack traces, and device, app-version, and diagnostic information to help identify and fix failures. Service availability depends on your version; this section also covers planned integrations in upcoming releases.

We do not include camera frames, saved images, image titles, or image categories in analytics events or crash-report attachments. Where a version offers analytics or diagnostics controls, those controls apply to future collection. Advertising-consent choices and device tracking permissions should not be assumed to disable every Firebase service. Contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning data we process.

5. Local storage, backups, and deletion

You can delete images from the app’s saved-images collection. This does not delete separate copies exported to your photo library; remove those through your device’s photo tools. Removing an exported photo does not necessarily remove its separate copy inside the app.

You can remove app-local data using your platform’s available storage-management options. Deleting the app and its data can remove locally saved items and preferences. We cannot recover or remotely delete images that remain only on your device.

Operating-system backups, device transfers, and cloud photo-library services may retain or synchronize app data or exported images according to your device settings. These services are controlled by your platform provider. Manage their copies and backups separately.

6. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to support and privacy requests and investigate problems. Please do not send identity documents, prescription information, financial details, or other sensitive images unless necessary for your request.

7. Service providers and retention

Our service providers include Google for advertising and, in versions that enable them, Firebase analytics and diagnostics. They may process information outside your country under their applicable policies and service settings.

Local saved items remain until you delete them or remove the app’s data. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle requests and meet applicable obligations. SDK information is retained according to the relevant service configuration and provider policies. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information already received by service providers.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps
  • Firebase privacy information

Third-party advertising destinations and external websites have their own privacy practices. Website visits to this policy are covered by our website privacy notice.

8. Security

Local information is kept in app storage or the photo library you select. SDK services use their own security measures for information they process. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Device access controls and backup settings also affect the privacy of saved images.

9. Children’s privacy

This app is intended as a general-purpose magnifying and mirror tool. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information to us can contact us so we can investigate and address the concern.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email us with the app name and a description of your request. We may need information to locate relevant records or verify the request.

Device permission controls, advertising choices, and local-data deletion serve different purposes. Removing local images does not by itself withdraw advertising consent or remove provider-held analytics information.

11. Policy updates and contact

We may revise this policy as features or service integrations change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For app support or privacy questions, contact Rice Awn LLC at contact@riceawn.com.

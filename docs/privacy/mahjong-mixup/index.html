PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Mahjong Mixup

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

Mahjong Mixup (“the App”) is developed and published by Rice Awn LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera access, microphone access, precise location, or payment information.

The App may store certain preferences locally on your device, such as tile style and sound settings. This information is not uploaded to our servers and may be removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements on both iOS and Android.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Information about ad impressions and interactions
  • Device and diagnostic information
  • Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention

Depending on your location, device settings, and applicable laws, Google may show personalized, non-personalized, limited, or contextual advertisements.

Google processes this information according to its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

More information about Google Mobile Ads data practices is available here:

https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Apple App Store and Google Play

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Apple and Google may independently process information related to app downloads, updates, device security, store accounts, payments, and store activity. We do not receive or control your full payment information.

This information is handled according to the applicable platform’s privacy policy:

Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

This Privacy Policy describes the information handled by Mahjong Mixup and its integrated services. It does not replace the privacy policies of Apple, Google, or other third-party services.

How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App or its advertising provider may be used to:

  • Operate and improve the App
  • Save local gameplay preferences
  • Deliver and measure advertisements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

The App may share information with Google AdMob and other service providers only as necessary to provide advertising, security, technical support, or other essential services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You may manage advertising preferences through your device settings and Google’s advertising controls. You may also reset or limit the use of your device’s advertising identifier where supported by your operating system.

The availability of privacy controls may vary depending on your device, operating system, location, and applicable laws.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information directly handled by us. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

Local App preferences remain on your device until they are deleted, the App is uninstalled, or the App’s data is cleared. Advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to Google’s policies.

Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements or links provided by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

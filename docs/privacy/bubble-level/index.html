PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

Bubble Level: Spirit & Angle

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Rice Awn LLC ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information in connection with the mobile application Bubble Level: Spirit & Angle (the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed as a utility tool that processes data locally on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Motion and Sensor Data (Core Tool Functionality)

  • The App requires access to your device's built-in motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) strictly to measure orientation, pitch, roll, and surface leveling in real time.
  • All sensor data is processed entirely on your device in real time. We do not store, record, transmit, or share your sensor data with any server or third party.

B. Personal Information

  • We do NOT collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or precise GPS location).
  • The App does not require user accounts or login credentials.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • We display banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob in the free version of the App.
  • AdMob may collect and process certain data, including:
  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Apple IDFA).
  • Coarse location data (derived from IP address).
  • App interaction and diagnostic/crash data for fraud detection and ad delivery.
  • For more details on how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

B. In-App Purchases (Remove Ads)

  • We offer an optional one-time In-App Purchase to remove all advertisements permanently.
  • All financial transactions and payment processing are handled directly and securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial billing information.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on external servers, your data is not susceptible to remote server breaches. All leveling calculations remain strictly on your local device.

6. Contact Us

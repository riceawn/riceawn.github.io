PRIVACY POLICY · RICE AWN LLC

A Tray of Trouble

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Provider: Rice Awn LLC
Contact: contact@riceawn.com

Rice Awn LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the mobile game A Tray of Trouble, identified by the package and bundle identifier com.riceawngames.atrayoftrouble.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use A Tray of Trouble.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

A Tray of Trouble does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The game may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Game settings and preferences
  • High scores and personal records
  • Gameplay progress
  • Whether the ad-free upgrade has been purchased

This locally stored information is used only to operate the game. Rice Awn LLC does not directly receive this information. It may be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app, subject to any device or platform backup features.

2. Advertising

A Tray of Trouble uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers
  • App launches, taps, ad views, and other interactions
  • Advertising data
  • Crash logs, performance information, and diagnostic data

Google may use this information to provide advertising, measure ad performance, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain the security of its services.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

You can manage or reset your advertising identifier through your device’s privacy settings. Purchasing the ad-free upgrade prevents the game from requesting in-game advertisements, but it does not necessarily delete information previously processed by Google.

Google’s privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases

A Tray of Trouble may offer a permanent in-app purchase that removes advertisements and unlocks additional gameplay benefits.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. Rice Awn LLC does not receive or store your payment card or bank account information.

The game may receive limited transaction information from the applicable store, such as:

  • Product identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Transaction identifier
  • Purchase receipt or purchase token

This information is used to confirm purchases, restore previously purchased content, prevent fraud, and provide the purchased features.

Purchase processing is also governed by the privacy policy of the applicable platform:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the game or its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the game
  • Save local game settings and progress
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Provide rewarded-ad features
  • Process and restore in-app purchases
  • Detect fraud and abuse
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Improve game performance and reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing of Information

Rice Awn LLC does not directly sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers as necessary to provide their services, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play
  • Apple App Store

Advertising partners may process information for personalized advertising where permitted by applicable law and the user’s consent or privacy settings.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

6. Data Retention

Information stored locally by the game remains on your device until it is deleted through the game, the device’s settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Third-party service providers retain information according to their own privacy policies, legal obligations, and retention practices.

7. Children’s Privacy

A Tray of Trouble is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

If the game is offered to children in a particular region, we will apply the additional protections required by applicable law and platform policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through the game. However, no electronic storage, transmission method, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Reset or delete your device’s advertising identifier
  • Limit ad personalization through device settings
  • Make advertising consent choices where available
  • Purchase the ad-free upgrade
  • Delete locally stored game data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it

Because A Tray of Trouble does not currently provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated user database, we generally cannot identify or retrieve an individual player’s locally stored game data.

For data processed by Google or Apple, please use the privacy controls and request mechanisms provided by those companies.

10. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those providers are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game’s features, service providers, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be published at this URL and the effective date at the top of the policy will be updated.

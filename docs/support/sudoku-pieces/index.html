Reporting an issue

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, and a brief description of what happened. If possible, describe the steps that led to the issue.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or unrelated personal information.

Purchases

If your question concerns a purchase, include the item name and the store used. Do not send your payment credentials.

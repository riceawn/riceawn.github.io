Dice Roller: 3D Simulator

Welcome to the support page for Dice Roller: 3D Simulator by Rice Awn LLC.

Contact Us

For help with the app, purchase issues, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, email:

contact@riceawn.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version
  • What happened and the steps to reproduce the issue
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if helpful

Please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Restore Purchases

To restore eligible permanent purchases, sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase, then open Settings → Restore purchases in the app.

If a purchase is not restored, check your internet connection and contact us with the product name and a description of the issue.

Billing and Refunds

Apple processes App Store payments. To request a refund or report a billing problem, visit:

https://reportaproblem.apple.com/

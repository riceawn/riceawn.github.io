Need a hand with a puzzle, a purchase, or something that isn’t working? We’re here to help.

Email: contact@riceawn.com

When reporting a problem, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS version and Jelly Mingle app version
  • A brief description of what happened
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

Restore a purchase

If you previously purchased Remove Ads, open Settings in Jelly Mingle and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.
